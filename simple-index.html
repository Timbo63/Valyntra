Most businesses don't need more tools

They need help choosing the right two or three, connecting them to a real workflow, putting reasonable controls in place, and making sure employees actually use them. That's the job.

Compete without an enterprise budget

You don't need a data science team to use AI well. You need to know which one or two changes would actually matter, and how to make them cheaply.

Spend less time on busywork

A lot of what slows small businesses down is repetitive — follow-ups, data entry, status updates. New tools can take that off your plate if they're set up right.

Make changes that actually stick

Plenty of small businesses have bought a tool that never got used. The plan only counts if your team actually adopts it — that's part of the job, not an afterthought.
